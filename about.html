About SS Apps Hub

Learn what this platform is, who it is for, and how to get the most from it.

What is SS Apps Hub?

SS Apps Hub is a centralised portal designed for Senior High School (SHS) teachers and students in Ghana. It provides quick, organised access to subject-specific learning apps built on the Playlab AI platform — covering all GES-approved subjects across Year 1 and Year 2.

Instead of searching for individual app links manually, users simply choose their year group and subject, then click "Go to App" to be taken straight to the relevant tool.

Who is it For?

  • SHS teachers who need interactive, AI-powered classroom tools
  • Students looking for subject-specific study and practice apps
  • School administrators seeking a single access point for digital resources
  • Anyone involved in Ghana's GES secondary education system

Lesson Plan Service

In addition to app access, SS Apps Hub offers a dedicated lesson plan ordering service for teachers. Our plans are:

  • Clean, professional, and ready to use
  • Tailored to specific subjects, year groups, and strands
  • Aligned with the GES Senior High School curriculum
  • Delivered quickly via WhatsApp
